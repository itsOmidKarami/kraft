Read Security and Data and privacy first: they say what Kraft does on your
machine and what leaves it. The rest is for people who run Kraft day to
day or work on its code.

## In this section

- [Security](/project/security): what Kraft does on your machine, the settings that make it safe to run, and its threat model.
- [Data and privacy](/project/data-and-privacy): what leaves your machine, and where Kraft keeps secrets and state.
- [Status and support](/project/status-and-support): which versions are supported, what the version numbers promise, and where to get help.
- [Architecture](/project/architecture): one FastAPI process and a React SPA on your machine, and how a work item moves through them.
- [Source map](/project/source-map): which module in `src/kraft/` holds what, and where to change a given thing.
- [Contributing](/project/contributing): the steps to contribute a change, and where the release process lives.
