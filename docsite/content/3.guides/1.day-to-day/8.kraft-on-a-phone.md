title: Kraft on a phone

Open Kraft's board on a phone and act on what needs you from it: the layout switches on a screen narrower than 768 pixels, at the same address as the desktop, and each card and each stopped item offers its own buttons.

## Before you start

Your phone must be able to reach the server. See [Remote access](/guides/run/remote-access) for how, and for signing in. The phone signs in the same way as the desktop.

## The layout

![The board at a 390px phone viewport, with bottom tab navigation](/assets/mobile.png){width="300"}

The layout switches on its own when the window is narrower than 768 pixels, with
no setting to turn on. It is the same app at the same address, so a link copied
on one opens on the other. Widening the window brings the desktop layout back on
the same page.

- A bar at the bottom holds **Board**, **Search**, [**Analytics**](/reference/web-ui/analytics#on-a-phone) and **More**.
- **More** lists everything else: Chains, Library, Repos and Harnesses, then
  Policy, Auto-intake, Notifications, Access, Appearance and About, then
  Analytics and **Archived**. An archived item opens from there, and **Restore**
  on the item puts it back on the board.
- The phone has no first-run wizard. With no repo connected, the board says
  "No repository is connected": connect one from a computer, or run
  `kraft repo connect` in the repo on the machine Kraft runs on.

A phone screen the desktop has no page for lands on the nearest desktop page,
when the window widens or a link sent from a phone opens on a laptop. **More**
lands on the board. One [harness](/concepts/vocabulary#harness), [agent profile](/concepts/vocabulary#agent-profile),
schedule or notification channel lands on its list, with the harness or agent
profile selected.

## The board on a phone

The board filters by **All**, **Needs you**, **Running**, **Not started** and
**Done**, the desktop board's groups. The **+** button files a new [work item](/concepts/vocabulary#work-item)
(**New work item**), as [File a work item](/guides/day-to-day/file-a-work-item)
describes.

A card that needs you carries its own buttons:

- **Approve** and **Reject…** for a gate;
- **Answer…** for a question;
- **Retry…** for a failed item;
- **Raise cap** for a cap and **Raise budget** for a dollar cap. Those two open
  the item with its raise sheet over it, or on its card, which says why, when
  the item can't raise it.

Tap the card for the item. **Approve** asks first, naming the node the item
moves on to, since a passed gate is not taken back. **Open the review** there
reads the gate's document instead.

After `kraft admin update` installs a release under a running server, the board
says so in a line under its filters, as the desktop's sidebar does. Restart to
finish the update. For an older release installed over a newer one, read About
first.

## An item on a phone

An item opens as its chain, node by node. Tap a node for its tasks, a task for
its log.

- **Review and decide** opens the gate's document and diff.
- Comments you added there and have not sent are counted as pending, beside the
  document's name too, and each is marked **pending**.
- A thread names its lines as the desktop does (`lines −2 to +6`) and quotes
  them. A thread on the whole change is listed above the files.
- **Approve** asks before it sends pending comments with the approval.
- An item that has not started offers **Start**. When its draft holds changes
  Start would not apply, Start lists them and asks first: **Apply and start**,
  or **Start without them**. A spec or plan attached when it was filed is listed
  under its title; tap one to read it.

A node's bar offers **Retry node** only once the item has stopped. On a paused
item it offers **Skip node** and **Resume**. A node waiting on CI offers
**Pause** and **Skip node**. A rate-limited node offers only **Pause**, so
pause it first to skip it.

## The bar on a stopped item

A stopped item has an action bar at the bottom with one or two buttons, chosen
by why it stopped:

| Why it stopped | The bar offers |
|---|---|
| At a gate | **Reject…** and **Review and decide** |
| A failure, or most other stops | **Escalate** and **Retry** |
| A question | **Escalate** and **Answer** |
| A merge conflict | **Cancel…** and **Review the conflicts** |
| A closed merge request | **Cancel…** and **Reopen MR** |
| A cap that names its limit | **Steer** and **Raise cap** |
| A cap that names no limit | **Steer** and **Retry** |
| A dollar cap the item can raise (its own cap, or the item-wide `budget_usd`) | **Raise budget** alone, with no **Steer** |
| A daily, token or node-level cap | **Retry** alone |
| An escalation running | **Retry**, which stops the escalation and retries the node |
| Waiting on a rate limit or on CI | **Pause** alone |
| Blocked behind an unfinished item, which its card lists | **Unblock** and **Pause** |

- **Steer** retries the node with your note. **Raise cap** opens a one-number
  sheet titled for that limit, such as **Raise the running time cap**.
- For a daily, token or node-level cap, the card says the item can't raise that
  cap, so raise it in the policy or the chain first.
- While an escalation runs, its node bar offers **Retry node**. Pause, Steer and
  Skip wait until the escalation answers.
- An item waiting on a rate limit or on CI is retried by Kraft itself. Its node
  reads **waiting on CI** or **waiting · rate limit**, not running.

## Check it worked

Open Kraft on your phone, or make the browser window narrower than 768 pixels.
The board shows the bottom bar with **Board**, **Search**, **Analytics** and
**More**, and a card under **Needs you** carries its own buttons.
