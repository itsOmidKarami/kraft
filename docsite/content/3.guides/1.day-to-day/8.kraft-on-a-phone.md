title: Kraft on a phone
description: The phone layout, with its bottom bar, the board's filters and cards, and the buttons on a stopped item.

Kraft shows a phone layout on a screen narrower than 768 pixels, at the same address as the desktop. To reach Kraft from your phone in the first place, see [Remote access](/guides/run/remote-access).

## On a phone

![The board at a 390px phone viewport, with bottom tab navigation](/assets/mobile.png)

On a screen narrower than 768 pixels Kraft shows its phone layout at the same
address. Widen the window to get the desktop back.

The phone has no first-run wizard. With no repo connected, the board says
"No repository is connected": connect one from a computer, or run
`kraft repo connect` in the repo on the machine Kraft runs on.

- A bar at the bottom holds **Board**, **Search**, **Analytics** and **More**.
  **More** lists Templates (Chains, Library), Settings (Repos, Harnesses,
  Policy, Auto-intake, Notifications, Access, Appearance, About), Analytics
  and **Archived**.
- The board filters by **All**, **Needs you**, **Running**, **Not started** and
  **Done**, the desktop board's groups. A card
  that needs you carries its own buttons, such as **Approve** and **Reject…**
  for a gate, or **Retry…** for a failed item. The **+** button files a new
  work item.
- An item opens as its chain, node by node: tap a node for its tasks, a task
  for its log.
- A stopped item has an action bar at the bottom with two buttons chosen by why
  it stopped: **Reject…** and **Review and decide** at a gate, **Escalate** and
  **Retry** for a failure, and so on.
  [On the phone](#on-the-phone), below, lists every stop and its buttons, and
  [Remote access](/guides/run/remote-access) how to reach Kraft from your phone.

## On the phone

Kraft switches to a phone layout on its own when the window is narrower than
768 pixels, with no setting to turn on. It is the same app at the same address,
signed in the same way, so a link copied on one opens on the other. Widening
the window brings the desktop layout back on the same page.

A phone screen
the desktop has no page for opens the nearest one: **More** opens the board,
and one harness, profile, schedule or notification channel opens its list,
with the harness or profile selected.

A bar at the bottom holds **Board**, **Search**, **Analytics** and **More**.
**More** lists everything else: Chains, Library, Repos and Harnesses, then
Policy, Auto-intake, Notifications, Access, Appearance and About, then
Analytics and **Archived**. An archived item opens from there, and **Restore**
on the item puts it back on the board.

- On the board, a card that needs you carries its own buttons:
  - **Approve** and **Reject…** for a gate;
  - **Answer…** for a question;
  - **Retry…** for a failed item;
  - **Raise cap** for a cap and **Raise budget** for a dollar cap. Those two open
    the item with its raise sheet over it, or on its card, which says why, when
    the item can't raise it.

  Tap the card for the item. **Approve** asks first, naming the node the item
  moves on to, since a passed gate is not taken back; **Open the review**
  there reads the gate's document instead.
- An item opens as its chain, node by node; tap a node for its tasks, a task
  for its log.
  - **Review and decide** opens the gate's document and diff.
  - Comments you added there and have not sent are counted as pending, beside
    the document's name too, and each is marked **pending**.
  - A thread names its lines as the desktop does (`lines −2 to +6`) and quotes
    them, and a thread on the whole change is listed above the files.
  - **Approve** asks before it sends pending comments with the approval.
- **New work item** files an item from the phone. An item that has not
  started offers **Start**. When its draft holds changes Start would not
  apply, Start lists them and asks first: **Apply and start**, or **Start
  without them**. A spec or plan attached when it was filed is
  listed under its title; tap one to read it.
- A stopped item's bar offers two actions, or one, by why it stopped:
  - a failed item, or most other stops: **Escalate** and **Retry**;
  - a question: **Escalate** and **Answer**;
  - a merge conflict: **Cancel…** and **Review the conflicts**;
  - a closed merge request: **Cancel…** and **Reopen MR**;
  - a cap that names its limit: **Steer** and **Raise cap**. **Steer**
    retries the node with your note. **Raise cap** opens a one-number sheet
    titled for that limit, such as **Raise the running time cap**. A cap that
    names none: **Steer** and **Retry**;
  - a dollar cap, with one button and no **Steer**: **Raise budget** when the
    item can raise it (its own cap, or the item-wide `budget_usd`). For a
    daily, token or node-level cap, **Retry**: the card says the item can't
    raise that cap, so raise it in the policy or the chain first.
- An item whose escalation is running offers **Retry**, which stops the
  escalation and retries the node; its node bar offers **Retry node**. Pause,
  Steer and Skip wait until the escalation answers.
- An item waiting on a rate limit or on CI offers only **Pause**: Kraft
  retries it by itself. Its node reads **waiting on CI** or **waiting · rate
  limit**, not running.
- After `kraft admin update` installs a release under a running server, the
  board says so in a line under its filters, as the desktop's sidebar does:
  restart to finish the update, or, for an older release installed over a
  newer one, read About first.
- A node's bar offers **Retry node** only once the item has stopped. On a
  paused item it offers **Skip node** and **Resume**. A node waiting on CI
  offers **Pause** and **Skip node**; a rate-limited one only **Pause**, so
  pause it first to skip it.
