## Before you start

Your phone must be able to reach the server. See [Remote access](/guides/run/remote-access) for how, and for signing in. The phone signs in the same way as the desktop.

## Open the board

1. Pick a way in on [Remote access](/guides/run/remote-access#choose-a-way) and restart Kraft.
2. Open that address in the phone's browser and sign in.

![The board at a 390px phone viewport, with bottom tab navigation, status chips and Chain, Gate and Stop reason menus, and cards for an item at a gate whose Review button opens the review, and stopped items with Retry and Raise cap](/assets/mobile.png){width="300"}

## Check it worked

Open Kraft on your phone, or make the browser window narrower than 768 pixels.
The board shows the bottom bar with **Board**, **Search**, **Analytics** and
**More**, and a card under **Needs you** carries its own buttons.
