The board's routes for a [work item](/concepts/vocabulary#work-item), beside the [stable routes](/reference/http-api#stable-routes): its status, overrides, cancelling, retrying and merge request. Routes written here without the `/api` prefix are relative to it, as [the HTTP API overview](/reference/http-api#in-this-section) explains.

Every other route under `/api/`, and the `/api/ws/events` WebSocket, exists
for the board and can change in any release. For those, prefer the `kraft`
command with `--json`, which is stable.

## `POST /api/work-items`

`POST /api/work-items`, the board's create route, files the item paused unless
the body sets `autostart: true`, like `kraft item create` without
`--autostart`.

With every slot busy (`max_concurrent`), an `autostart: true`
item is filed paused anyway, and the answer carries
`"slots": {"busy": 3, "limit": 3}` to say why.

On a repo that declares no setup command, or no test
command nor test scopes, the answer carries `repo_warning`, saying where the item will stop.

The chain is named by:

- `chain`; `chain_template`,
  the pre-2.0 name, is still read, on `PATCH` too, and an unknown one is
  refused.
- A body naming two different chains under the two names is
  refused (`422`, its `detail` one sentence naming both); the same one twice
  is fine.
- A body with neither gets the
  repo's `default_chain`.

Every repo path you send must be absolute: the
server would read a relative one against its own directory, not yours.
`POST /api/repos`, `POST /api/work-items` and `POST /api/triggers` refuse it
with a `422`, and `PATCH` or `DELETE /api/repos?path=` answer `404`.

## Work item status

`GET /api/work-items` and `GET /api/work-items/{id}` both carry `display_status` and `stop` on every item. The detail adds `test_result`. All three are read-only and computed on read rather than stored (see
[Displayed status](/reference/statuses#displayed-status)):

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `display_status` | The board's status badge: one of `archived`, `done`, `cancelled`, `paused`, `running`, `waiting`, `needs_you`, `escalated`, `failed`. |
| `stop` | `null` unless the item is `needs_human`, `waiting` or `rate_limited`. See [The `stop` field](#the-stop-field). |
| `test_result` | Detail only. The latest changed-test-scope verification run, `{scopes: [{command, scope, passed, exit_code, session_id}], passed}`, or `null` when no scope has finished. See [The `test_result` field](#the-test_result-field). |

### `stop.kind`

`stop.kind` is one of twelve values: `gate`, `question`, `cap`, `budget`, `failed`, `conflict`, `mr_closed`, `config`, `infra`, `stuck`, `wait` and `rate_limit`. [Stop kinds](/reference/events#stop-kinds) says what each means, and [Why did my item stop?](/troubleshooting/why-did-my-item-stop#find-your-reason) gives each stop's reason with its fix.

A `rate_limit` stop's `facts` also carries `fallback` and `fallback_allowed`: the stopped task's declared fallback [harnesses](/concepts/vocabulary#harness), and which of those its resolved policy still allows.

A `needs_human` item stopped on 1.4.0 or earlier has no recorded kind. It reads `question`, `budget` or `cap` where its stop event carries one, else `failed`. A `waiting` row reads `wait` and a `rate_limited` one `rate_limit`.

### The `stop` field

On the list, `stop` is `{kind, node, resume_at, reason}`; on the detail, also `task`, `attempt` and `facts`.

#### `facts.cause`

`facts.cause` names why an `infra` stop happened, when Kraft knows. [The `facts` field](/reference/events#the-facts-field) lists the values. The failed card chooses its action from it, and an `infra` stop without one reads as a generic infrastructure stop.

#### `limit`

A `cap` or `budget` stop on the detail also carries `limit: {path, key, value, maximum}` when a `PATCH` of the item's `policy` can raise what stopped it, and omits it when none can.

- `key` is `max_attempts` or `timeout_minutes` (a [fix loop](/concepts/vocabulary#fix-loop)'s attempts or its clock, `path` the node id), or `time_cap_minutes` or `total_time_cap_minutes` (the work item's own cap, `path` `""`), or, on a `budget` stop, `budget_usd` (the item-wide policy dollar cap, `path` `""`).
- `value` is the limit that stopped it, in attempts, minutes or dollars.
- `maximum` is the `maxima` ceiling or `null`.

Raise it with `PATCH /work-items/{id}` `{policy: {<key>: N}}` (item-wide) or `{policy: {paths: {<path>: {<key>: N}}}}`, then `POST /work-items/{id}/retry`.

There is no `limit` for:

- a `budget` stop on the item's own cap (`POST /work-items/{id}/budget/raise` raises that one), on a node, step or task's `budget_usd`, a `token_budget`, the daily cap or spend a harness never reported;
- a node, step or task's time cap;
- a gate's or wait's timeout;
- a `loops:` cap in `policy.yaml`;
- exhausted rate-limit retries;
- a fix loop whose bound the older `node_overrides` set.

#### `scope`

A `budget` stop on the detail also carries `scope`, the cap that stopped it:

| `scope` | The cap |
|---|---|
| `work_item` | The item's own dollar cap. |
| `usd` | A `budget_usd`, item-wide or on a node, step or task, or spend a harness never reported; `POST /work-items/{id}/budget/raise` takes `work_item` and an item-wide `usd`. |
| `tokens` | A `token_budget`. |
| `daily` | `budget.daily_usd`. |

### The `test_result` field

- One entry per scope, passed or failed (`scope` is `null` when the repo's table no longer declares the command, `exit_code` when none was recorded); `passed` is true when every scope passed.
- An area's setup appears only when it failed.
- A scope still running is left out.
- No test counts.
- `session_id` is the scope's session; read its log with `GET /api/worker-sessions/{id}/log`.

## Overrides


`agent_overrides`, `node_overrides` and `policy_override` are objects in
`GET /api/work-items/{id}` and in every answer that hands back the item itself:
a gate's approve or reject, `complete`, `cancel`, `skip`, and a `retry` or
`resume` that stops at once. `agent_overrides` and `policy_override` are
`null` when the item has none, and `node_overrides` is `{}`.

`PATCH /api/work-items/{id}` with `agent_overrides` replaces the item's whole
model and effort override. A field you leave out, or send as
`null`, is dropped, and `{}` clears the override. The answer echoes the
override as stored: `{}` once it is cleared.

`node_overrides` merges per node
instead: `{"plan": {"effort": "high"}}` sets that field and keeps the node's
others, `{"plan": {"effort": null}}` drops one field, and `{"plan": {}}` drops
the node's overrides. The answer echoes each node you named, as stored:
`{"plan": {}}` once you clear it.

A node that has started answers `409`,
except on an item stopped at that node because its stored `model` or
`escalate_model` is not a model id: there `{}`, and a change to `model`,
`escalate_model` or `effort` alone, are accepted.

## Cancelling

`GET /work-items/{id}/cancel-preview` shows what `POST /work-items/{id}/cancel`
would do, without doing it: `409` once the item has already ended, same as
`/cancel` itself.

```json
{
  "running": {"node": "mr_checks", "task": "mr_checks", "attempt": 1, "started_at": "2026-10-03T10:00:00+00:00"} | null,
  "kept": {"branch": "kraft/...", "worktree": "/path", "findings": 0, "threads": 1},
  "mr": {"ref": 54, "url": "https://...", "state": "open"} | null,
  "spend": {"spent_usd": 1.2, "cap_usd": 5.0},
  "beads": ["kraft-ab1"]
}
```

- `running` is the item's running session, if any, and when it started.
- `kept` is what cancel leaves
  behind (cancel never removes the [worktree](/concepts/vocabulary#worktree) or the branch, and archive later
  removes the worktree but keeps a branch with commits nowhere else).
- `mr` is
  the item's merge request and its live state, or `null` before one was ever
  opened.
- `beads` is every bead `POST /work-items/{id}/complete` closes when
  asked with `close_beads`: the item's own bead, those it implements, and those
  a `Fixes` or `Closes` trailer on its own commits names (an id starting
  `Kraft-`, any case). An item filed while `bd` was down has none yet, and
  `close_beads` files one for it and closes that.

`POST /work-items/{id}/cancel` takes an optional `close_mr: bool = false`.
When set and the item's merge request is open, cancel closes it on the [forge](/concepts/vocabulary#forge)
once the cancel itself has landed and the response gains `close_mr: {ok:
true}` (or `{ok: false, error}` when there was no open merge request or the
forge call failed). A failed close never undoes the cancel. The key is
absent when `close_mr` was not asked for.

`POST /work-items/{id}/archive` answers `{"id", "archived_by",
"worktree_removed"}`. It adds `kept_branch` and `unpushed_commits` when it
kept the item's branch because some of its commits are on no other branch,
remote-tracking branch or tag, and not in what Kraft pushed.

It adds
`rescued_branch` (`kraft/rescued/<id>`) and `rescued_commits` when the
worktree's HEAD was detached on commits no branch has. If that branch cannot
be made, the item is still archived but its worktree stays, and the answer
has `worktree_kept: "rescue failed: <reason>"`.

Archive keeps the
attachment copies.

When the item's repository has been moved or deleted, archive and
`POST /work-items/{id}/abandon` remove the worktree directory directly and
add `repo_missing`, the repository path they could not find. Nothing is done
to the branch: if you only moved the repository, it is still there.

## Retrying, duplicating, acting in bulk

`POST /work-items/{id}/retry`'s response gains `attempt`: the attempt number
the task it relaunches will run as: the count of that (item, node, task)'s
own sessions so far, plus one. A `path` naming one task counts only that
task's sessions; a `path`-less retry (the whole stopped node, or `restart`'s
whole chain) takes the highest next-attempt over all of the node's own tasks,
since a [walk](/concepts/vocabulary#walk) may enter at any one of them.

`POST /work-items/{id}/duplicate` files a fresh, paused item from `{id}`'s own
title, description, repo, chain, workspace selection and
attachments (re-snapshotted under the new item's own id). Any source status is
accepted, archived and cancelled included. No run state, override, policy
override, budget or bead link carries over. This is a fresh intake, not a
clone of the row.

```json
{"id": "...", "status": "paused", "duplicate_warning": "..."}
```

`duplicate_warning` is present only when another open item looks like a
duplicate, the same check `POST /work-items` makes. A source whose stored
attachment copy has gone missing, as it does when the source is abandoned,
answers `409` naming it, before anything is filed.

`POST /work-items/bulk` applies one action to several items at once:

```json
{"action": "pause", "ids": ["...", "..."], "reason": "..."}
```

`action` is one of `pause`, `cancel`, `archive`, `restore`. Each is the same door
the single-item route already has, called once per id, each in its
own write, in `ids` order. `reason` is required, and checked before anything
is touched, when `action` is `cancel` (`422` otherwise); the other three
ignore it. The response is one result per id, in order:

```json
{"results": [{"id": "...", "ok": true, "status": "paused"}, {"id": "...", "ok": false, "error": "..."}]}
```

An id whose action raises (an item in the wrong status, say) becomes `{id,
ok: false, error, status}` (no `status` for an id naming no item) and the rest
of the batch still runs; an id naming no work item answers `{id, ok: false, error: "not found"}`. `status` is the item's
stored status after the action ran.

## MR closed externally

A poller checks every `waiting`/`needs_human` item parked at a merge-request
node against the forge every `policy.forge_poll_s` seconds (default 300). When
the merge request was closed there without merging, it stops the item with
`stop.kind: "mr_closed"` and `facts: {ref, url}` (see [Events →
work_item_needs_human](/reference/events#work_item_needs_human)).

`POST /work-items/{id}/reopen-mr` reopens that merge request on the forge and
retries the stopped node, the same as `POST /work-items/{id}/retry` with no
`path`. It answers `409` unless the item is actually stopped with
`stop.kind: "mr_closed"`, and `502` with the forge's own message when the
forge call fails (the item stays stopped). Its response is `/retry`'s own
response shape. "Open a new MR" instead is `/retry` with the MR node's path;
cancelling the item instead is `/cancel`.

Cancelling leaves the closed merge request and the worktree as they are. [MR closed externally](/reference/statuses#mr-closed-externally) lists the three ways out and the board's button for each.
