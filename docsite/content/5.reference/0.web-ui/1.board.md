The board lists every work item, grouped by what it needs from you. This page names its groups, a row's buttons, the filters, and the side panel that opens when you click a row.

![The Kraft board: work items grouped by Needs you, Running, Not started, and Done](/assets/board.png)

The board lists every work item in four groups:

- **Needs you**: a gate waits for your approval, an agent asked a question,
  the item stopped on a failure or a cap, or a person paused it mid-chain. It
  always comes first, and the header shows how many with **N NEED YOU**. The
  sidebar's **Board**, an item page's **N others need you** and the phone's
  **Board** tab count the same items.
- **Running**: an agent or a check is working on it.
- **Not started**: filed paused and never started.
- **Done**: completed and cancelled items. It shows the five most recent;
  **show all N** shows the rest.

Each row shows the title, then the repo, the item's ID, its chain, how long
ago it changed, and why it is where it is (`approve spec`,
`failed at implementation`, `created paused`). The ticks on the right are the
chain's nodes, filled in as the item moves along it. A row that needs
something carries one button:

| The row says | It does |
|---|---|
| **Review to approve** | opens the item's review page at the waiting gate, with the gate's brief open beside the diff |
| **Answer** | opens the side panel at the agent's question |
| **Raise cap** | for a time or attempts cap: opens the side panel's Config tab, which has no editor for that cap ([how to open the editor](#raise-cap-on-a-row)) |
| **Retry…** | for a failed item or a stuck fix loop: opens the side panel, where Retry is |
| **Resume** | resumes a paused item that has already started |
| **Raise budget** | for a dollar or token budget stop: opens the side panel, and [which view opens](#raise-budget-on-a-row) depends on the stop |
| **Open** | opens the side panel for any other stop |

A row that has not started has no button. Start it from the side panel.

## Raise cap on a row

Click the panel's **Raise cap**, its main button at the bottom or the one in
Overview's banner, to open the cap's editor.

## Raise budget on a row

What opens depends on the stop:

- **A policy `budget_usd` cap**: the side panel's Overview, with the **Raise
  budget cap** dialog open.
- **The item's own cap**: the Config tab, with the budget editor open.
- **A budget the item cannot raise** (the day's cap, say): the panel stays on
  Overview, whose banner says so. The panel's main button is then **Retry**.

## Filter, Group and Sort

- **Filter by title or id** narrows every group as you type. Press **/** from
  anywhere on the board to jump to it.
- **Chain ▾** shows only items on one chain.
- The **all repos ▾** menu beside **Board** at the top shows only one repo.
- **Group** sorts the rows into groups by **Status** (the default), **Repo**
  or **Chain**. **Needs you** comes first in all three.
- **Sort** orders the rows within a group: **Needs attention**, **Recently
  updated**, **Created** or **Title**.

The filter, group and sort are kept in the page address, so a link you share
opens the same view.

## Selecting several items

Tick a row's checkbox (or press **Space** on a row) to select it. A bar
appears at the bottom with what you can do to the selection:

- **‖ Pause N** pauses the selected items that are running.
- **Cancel N…** cancels every selected item that has not ended. It asks for a
  reason first, and the reason goes into each item's run log. For a few
  seconds after you confirm, **Undo** in the message at the bottom takes it
  back.
- **Archive N** archives the selected items that are completed or cancelled.
  It is greyed out until you select one of those.
- **Clear** drops the selection.

**Select all** on the **Done** header selects the finished items the group is
showing (the newest few, five by default, or all of them after **show all N**),
so **Archive** clears those off the board in one go. If some of the items refuse
the action, the bar lists each one with its reason and keeps them selected.

## Archived items

The **Done** header carries a link, **completed and cancelled · auto-archive
after 30 days** (the number is `archive.after_days` in
[`policy.yaml`](/reference/configuration/policy)). It opens **Archived**, at
`/archived`, which lists every archived item. Filter it, sort it by
**Recently archived**, **Created** or **Title**, and click **Restore** on a
row to put it back on the board. Tick several rows for **Restore N**. On a
phone, **Archived** is under **More**: open an item there to restore it.

## The side panel

Click a row and a panel opens on the right, over the list, without leaving
the board. Its heading shows the item's title, and under it the item's ID,
its group and why it is there. It
has three tabs:

- **Overview**: the item's state card with what it is waiting on and the
  buttons to act on it (approve, answer, retry, raise a cap, escalate), and a
  short view of the chain with the node it is at. A stuck fix loop's card
  gives its reason, **Retry from** the task, and **Escalate…**.
- **Activity**: every event on the item, newest first. **Show earlier** loads
  more. Click an event about a node to open that node.
- **Config**: the limits the item runs under (its budget, tokens, the day's
  spend across all items) and anything changed for this item alone. The pencil
  beside **Budget** edits the item's dollar cap.

The bottom of the panel holds the item's main action, the same as the item
page's header (on [An item's page](/reference/web-ui/item-page)). **Open item ↗** and **Focus** both open the item's
full page.

To skip the panel, **⌘-click** (**Ctrl-click**) or double-click a row. Set
**Settings › Appearance › Board › Open items in** to **Full page** to make a
plain click do that too.

- **↑** and **↓** move between rows.
- **Esc**, the button at the panel's top right, or a click anywhere outside it
  closes the panel.
- While it is open, **Tab** from the selected row goes into the panel and on to
  the next row after it. The row buttons the panel covers are skipped, as a
  pointer cannot reach them either.
