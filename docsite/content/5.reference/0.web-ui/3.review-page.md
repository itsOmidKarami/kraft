The review page shows an item's diff with a bar along the bottom that holds your review. This page names its parts: comparing, marking files viewed, commenting on a line, finishing a review and the page at a gate. For what each outcome does, see [Reviewing a change](/guides/day-to-day/review-a-change).

![The review page: the file tree on the left, a diff of calc.py, and a comment being written on lines −2 to +2 (the removed line and its replacement) with Must fix selected, above the Your review bar with Finish review](/assets/review.png)

**Review changes** opens the review page at `/work-items/<id>/review`. You can
open it at any point in the item's life, not only at a gate. The file tree is
on the left, the diff on the right, and a bar along the bottom holds your
review.

## Compare

The toolbar reads **Compare** *from* **and** *to*:

- **Compare from**: **base**, the item's starting point; an earlier
  attempt; or **your last review**, to see only what changed since you last
  submitted one.
- **Compare to**: **latest (attempt N)**, the current state of the branch, or
  an earlier attempt.

Attempts are listed while a gate waits for you.

**All nodes ▾** limits the diff to the files chosen nodes touched. On the
right, the toolbar collapses or expands every file, and the list button holds
the diff settings: **Side-by-side** or **Inline**, whitespace changes, changed
words, line wrapping, and one file at a time.

## Viewed

Each file's header has **Viewed**. Tick it once you have read the file: the
tree marks it, and the foot of the tree counts how many you have viewed out of
the total. Each header also has a button to comment on the whole file, and a
**⋮** menu with **Copy diff** and **Copy path**.

## Commenting on a line

Hover a line and click the **+** that appears at the left of its number. To
comment on a few lines, drag the **+** down to the last one you want; the
lines shade as you go. A drag stays inside one hunk of the diff. For a longer
range, click the first line's **+** or number, then **Shift**-click the last
one's. Dragging down the numbers picks a range too. **c** or **Enter** also
opens the box on a pick.

Each side has its own lines. In **Side-by-side**, each half has its own **+**.
In **Inline**, an unchanged line has two numbers. To comment on the old file's
line, click the left number, then **+**. To comment on the new file's line,
click the right number, then **+**.

A range can run across sides, as GitLab's and GitHub's do. Drag from a removed
line's **+** down to the line that replaced it, or **Shift**-click it. In
**Side-by-side**, drag or **Shift**-click across to the right half. The range
then covers every line between its two ends.

A comment box opens under the last line. Its header names the lines, marked as
the diff marks them: **Line +5**, **Lines +5 to +7**, **Lines −2 to +2**. The
thread it makes is headed the same way.

### Moving the range

While the box is open, the shaded range has a small handle on its first and last
rows.

- Drag one to move that end, even past the other end, which flips the range.
- **Shift**-click another line, or press **Shift+↑** or **Shift+↓**, to move the
  end too.
- The box keeps what you wrote, and **×** next to the lines goes back to the
  last line alone.

A suggested change you typed moves with the range, and the box says which lines
it was written for; on a range across sides it is set aside, and not sent, until
the start is back on the new side.

### After you add the comment

- Once you add the comment, its thread stays under the last line, headed with the
  lines it covers.
- A thread on no file, such as one from `kraft item comment --body` alone, is
  listed above the files under **On the whole change**.
- A range's lines stay shaded and are quoted in the thread, and the agent that
  reads the thread gets the same quote, as it does for one made with
  `kraft item comment` or an agent's `add_review_comment`, which Kraft quotes
  from the diff.

The pick clears when the box closes.

### In the comment box

- Pick a label: **No label**, **Must fix**, **Question** or **Nit**. What a
  **Must fix** label blocks is in
  [Must-fix](/guides/day-to-day/review-a-change#must-fix).
- Write the comment. Markdown works, and **Preview** shows it rendered.
- **± Suggest change**, on lines of the new side, adds the lines you picked,
  for you to edit into the code you want. Left as it was, it is not sent. It
  is off for a range that runs across lines the diff does not show, and a range
  across sides has none.
- **Add to review**, or **⌘↵** (**Ctrl+Enter**), keeps it as a draft. Drafts
  reach no agent until you finish the review. **Cancel** or **Esc** asks
  before it throws away what you wrote.

### From the keyboard

Focus a file's lines and use **↑** and **↓** to pick a
line, **Shift+↑** and **Shift+↓** to extend the pick, **c** or **Enter** to
comment, and **n** and **p** to move to the next or previous file. **Tab**
from the lines reaches the picked line's **+**. **Shift** with the arrows keeps
to one side, so a range that crosses sides is made with the mouse: the handles
or **Shift**-click.

## Finish review

The bar at the bottom reads **Your review** with your drafts counted:
**N pending, N must fix, N unlabeled**. **Finish review** opens **Finish your
review**, which lists the drafts it will send, takes an optional overall note,
and offers three outcomes. [Reviewing a change](/guides/day-to-day/review-a-change#drafts-and-sending)
says what each one does.

- **Request changes**: the dialog shows where the work will restart, the nodes
  that run after it, and which fix round this would be (**Fix round N of M**),
  before you submit. It reads the same target the submit acts on, so the two
  cannot differ.
  - At a gate the dialog reads **Reject *gate* · goes back to *node***, and its
    button reads **Reject to *node***.
  - When no fix rounds are left, the dialog says **No fix rounds left** and
    **Request changes** is disabled.
- **Comment only** sends the threads without stopping anything. Agents answer
  questions in the thread; at a gate, the gate stays open.
- **Approve**: only offered at a gate, where it does the same as
  [Approve on the gate](/reference/web-ui/item-page#a-gate-that-is-waiting). It
  stays greyed out while a must-fix thread is open.

**Submit review** sends it (at a gate, with **Request changes** chosen, the
button reads **Reject to *node*** instead).

Once a review has gone out:

- The bar reads **N of M resolved** and offers **Request changes** and
  **Approve** directly.
- A comment you add after that shows there as pending, **Finish review** comes
  back beside them, and **Approve** opens **Finish your review** first, so a new
  comment is never sent with an approval unseen.

[Reviewing a change](/guides/day-to-day/review-a-change#in-the-web-ui) lists the CLI verb
behind each of these.

## At a gate

**Review changes** from a waiting gate opens the review page with the gate's
document first, rendered, and the gate along the top: its name, **WAITING FOR
YOU**, how many comments are pending, and **Request changes** and **Approve**.
The changed files are listed on the right; **Review changes** below them
switches to the diff.
