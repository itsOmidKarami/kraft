The Analytics screen rolls up where the time and the money went across your [work items](/concepts/vocabulary#work-item). This page says what each figure, chart and table counts. Open it from **Analytics** in the sidebar.

![The Analytics screen for items created in the last 8 weeks: the Range, Repo and Chain filters, the five Completed, Lead time, Waiting on you, Cost and Repairs per item figures with a line of detail under each, a Throughput by week bar chart, the Waiting at each gate table, and the top of the By node table](/assets/analytics.png)

## What the screen counts

- **The items.** Every figure covers the work items created in the chosen range (8 weeks by default), narrowed by the filters. An item created earlier is left out, even if it merged yesterday. Only **Completed**, **Lead time**, **Waiting on you** and the two figures under the stops are limited to completed items.
- **Their history.** For each of those items, the screen adds up all its [worker sessions](/concepts/vocabulary#worker-session) and events, whenever they happened.
- **A sliced item counts once.** An item built in [slices](/concepts/how-a-work-item-runs#slices) is one item here; its slices' sessions and events add to the item's cost, tokens, repairs, merges, stops and time waiting. **Lead time** stays the item's own, so **Waiting on you** can count slices that waited at the same time and stops at 100%.
- **No stored totals.** Kraft computes the figures from the database each time you open the screen or change a filter.

## Filters

All three apply to every panel and reset when you leave the screen.

| Menu | Narrows to |
|---|---|
| **Range ▾**: **7 days**, **30 days**, **8 weeks** (default) or **90 days** | The items created in that long before now |
| **Repo ▾** | The items filed in one repo |
| **Chain ▾** | The items on one [chain](/concepts/vocabulary#chain). An item that never chose one counts as `default`; one filed with a one-off chain counts under **one-off** _Added in 2.5_ |

## Overview

| Figure | What it counts | The line under it |
|---|---|---|
| **Completed** | Items whose status is `completed` | `+N vs previous`: the change from the range before |
| **Lead time** | The median, over completed items, from the chain's first node starting to completion. A wait after the merge, such as `post_merge_ci`, counts; a wait behind a dependency doesn't | `median` |
| **Waiting on you** | The time completed items spent stopped on you, as a share of their lead time | `of lead time` |
| **Cost** | The cost recorded for every [worker](/concepts/vocabulary#worker) session of every item in the set, completed or not | `$X per completed item`, or `nothing completed yet` |
| **Repairs per item** | The repair rounds below, over items that needed at least one | `N hit their cap`, or `none hit their cap` |

Time waiting on you runs from a gate being requested, or an item stopping to wait for you, until the gate is approved or rejected, the item is retried or a [node](/concepts/vocabulary#node) starts. It is kept apart from run time, so a slow reviewer doesn't read as a slow agent.

### Repairs and caps

- **Repairs per item** counts the distinct repair rounds that ran a worker session in the items' [fix-loop](/concepts/vocabulary#fix-loop) nodes, over the items with at least one. A repair round is round `1` or higher. A node's first pass (round `0`) is no repair.
- **N hit their cap** counts the items with a fix-loop session that ended at its attempt limit. A wait that ran out and a time [cap](/concepts/vocabulary#cap) don't count.

### A trailing plus

A trailing `+` (`$1.16+`) marks a sum that is missing a session: one with tokens and no dollar figure, because the [harness](/concepts/vocabulary#harness) reports no cost and its model isn't in Kraft's price table, or a resumed session's tokens couldn't be separated from the earlier session's. The true total is higher. [Cost reporting](/reference/harnesses/cost-reporting) says which harnesses report no cost.

Kraft's own estimate from the price table is added when the model is listed, unmarked: for a session still running (a floor, since it leaves out output tokens) and for a finished one whose agent reported no cost.

## Throughput by week

A bar chart of merged items per week: eight columns, oldest first, whatever the range. The label under a bar is the Monday that starts the week, in UTC. The dashed bar is the current week, and partial.

A merge is a node finishing that runs a [forge](/concepts/vocabulary#forge) task with the target `mr.merge`, such as the shipped `merge` node. A completed item whose chain has no such node, such as `quick-task`, completes without showing up. When nothing merged, the chart reads **No merge requests merged in this range.**

## Waiting at each gate

_Added in 2.1_

One row per [gate](/concepts/vocabulary#gate) that stopped an item on a person, longest total wait first.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **share of wait** | The gate's total wait against the longest |
| **waits**, **median**, **total** | How many times an item stopped there, and the median and sum of those waits |
| **rejected** | How many times the gate was rejected, by you or by Kraft's own review |

A stop that is no gate, such as a failure that needs you, is left out here, though it counts in **Waiting on you**.

## By node

One row per node that ran a worker session, highest cost first.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **share of cost**, **%** | The node's cost against the costliest node, and as a share of all rows |
| **min** | The mean wall time of one worker session on the node, in whole minutes; `<1 min` under a minute |
| **tokens** | Every token its sessions used: uncached input, cache writes, cache reads and output |
| **$** | The cost of its sessions; a trailing `+` marks a missing session |

## By repo

One row per repo, the one with the most items first: **items** created in the range in any status, **done** (completed), **cost** and **avg repairs**, the **Overview** figure for that repo alone.

## Why items stopped for a person

One bar per reason, most common first. A count is a number of stops, not of items: an item that stops three times adds three. For what each stop means and its fix, see [Why did my item stop?](/troubleshooting/why-did-my-item-stop).

| Label | A stop where |
|---|---|
| `gate · <name>` | A gate named `<name>` was requested |
| `budget` | A spend cap stopped the item |
| `cap breach` | A [fix loop](/concepts/vocabulary#fix-loop) reached its attempt cap |
| `orchestrator/infra defect` | The reason starts `resume: current-node session did not resolve cleanly` |
| `preventable dispatch` | The reason starts `needs_context:` and contains `blocked on`, `not merged` or `depends on` |
| `task failure` | Any other stop that waits on you |

Under the bars, two figures cover completed items only:

- **N unplanned touches per item**: the stops nobody designed the chain to need, divided by the completed items. A `gate · ...` stop never counts. `budget`, `orchestrator/infra defect` and `preventable dispatch` always do. `cap breach` and `task failure` do unless Kraft cleared the stop without paging you: an automatic escalation retry, or a gate its own review decided.
- **open MR → checks done X median**: the median time from the node that opens the draft merge request starting to the node that waits on CI completing, found by their forge tasks (`mr.open_draft`, `mr.ci`) and not their IDs. `—` when no item has both.

## How this is counted

_Added in 2.1_

Each repo in **By repo**, each gate in **Waiting at each gate** and the **gate · …**, **budget** and **cap breach** reasons open the [board](/reference/web-ui/board) filtered to it, keeping the repo and chain set here. The board shows items as they are now, so a gate nobody waits at today opens an empty board.

The other reasons open it for the repo and chain only. The closed **How this is counted** line at the bottom has the notes for the whole screen.

## On a phone

**Analytics** is a tab in the bottom bar, with the same figures as tiles and lists, no weekly chart, and **Range**, **Repo** and **Chain** as buttons under the title. See [Kraft on a phone](/guides/day-to-day/kraft-on-a-phone). When nothing has completed in the range and no node ran, the figures give way to **Nothing has completed in this range yet.**
