An item's page shows one [work item](/concepts/vocabulary#work-item): its brief, its [chain](/concepts/vocabulary#chain) drawn as a graph, and an inspector for whatever you select. This page covers the main action, the header's actions, the graph and the inspector's tabs.

![An item's page: a banner reading Waiting for you to approve final review with a Review button, the chain drawn as a graph of named nodes with the final_review gate selected, the chain overview above it (a glyph per node, framed on the part in view), and the inspector on the right showing the gate's files, an open thread, and the Read kraft-cb59-review.md, Review gate, Approve and Request changes buttons](/assets/item.png)

## The page

An item's page, at `/work-items/<id>`, has the title and brief at the top (**edit** changes the brief) and, under it, a line counting the changed files and open [review threads](/concepts/vocabulary#review-thread), with a **Review changes** link. When the item needs you, a banner says so, such as **Waiting for you to approve spec** with **Review**, which opens the review page at that [gate](/concepts/vocabulary#gate).

An item that never started reads **NOT STARTED**, never **Paused**.

## The main action

Each state has one main action, next to the words that explain the state: in the banner or the card under the brief. A state with neither keeps it in the header. The header also shows the status, the **⋮** menu, and how long the item has been going since its chain's first node started (a wait behind a dependency doesn't count).

| The item | Main action | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Never started | **Start** | Header |
| Running or waiting | **Pause** | Header |
| Paused | **Resume**; **Resume with steer** once a steer is typed | Paused card |
| At a gate | **Review**: opens the review page at the gate | Banner |
| Asking a question | **Send & resume**, or **Open thread** | Question card |
| Stopped at a [cap](/concepts/vocabulary#cap) or a budget it can raise | **Raise cap**: opens the editor for the limit the stop names. **Open config** when it names none. See [Raise a cap](/guides/day-to-day/raise-a-cap#which-stop-offers-what) | Banner |
| A budget it can't raise, or an escalation running | **Retry** | Header |
| [Stuck](/concepts/vocabulary#stuck) or failed | **Retry from** the stopped task, with a steer box | State card |
| Rebase conflict | **Review the conflicts** | State card |
| Merge request closed | **Reopen !N** | State card |
| Cancelled | **Duplicate as new item**, **Archive** | State card |
| Done | **Archive**, under the outcome | State card |
| Archived | **Restore** | Header |

For why a failed item stopped and what to do, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).

## What an item comes after

_Added in 2.1_

An item filed to come after others lists the unfinished ones in a card under the brief: each one's status and title. On a [blocked](/reference/statuses) item the card is titled **Blocked**, and Kraft starts the item when they have all completed. **Unblock** stops it waiting for them; the card offers it on a blocked, paused or not-started item.

## Header actions

A header button runs when you select its name. Its **▾** opens a menu with that action first, then the others that fit the state. Where the banner or card carries the main action, the **⋮** menu ends with the same items.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Escalate…** | Asks an agent to help with a stop. Offered on an item stopped or paused after it started, not at a budget stop (raise the cap first), or while a session runs on its pending gate or an escalation runs |
| **Mark complete…**, **Cancel…** | Say before you confirm what they stop (a running attempt's work is lost), what they keep (branch, [worktree](/concepts/vocabulary#worktree), findings, run log) and what the status becomes |
| **Duplicate as new item** | Files a copy once the item has ended |
| **Item settings** | Opens the chain's Config tab |

Once an escalation thread exists, **Start a new thread** begins one that doesn't see the earlier turns. The **⋮** menu also holds **Review changes**, **Open worktree in editor**, **Copy ID** and **Copy link**. Select the title to rename the item.

## The chain graph

The middle of the page draws the item's chain, frozen when the item was filed, [node](/concepts/vocabulary#node) by node. A box is an agent or a command; a diamond is a gate. Each node shows how it went and how long it took, and a gate that sends work back draws a **reject** arc to the node it re-runs.

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **×N** on a node | The most times any one of its tasks ran, by a retry or a fix-loop round. Red on a node stopped at its cap |
| A number, and a red **×**, in a task's corner | Claude Code sub-agents running now, and one that failed, was stopped or was cut off |
| A row of marks under a task | One per sub-agent: a dot running, a tick finished, a cross when it didn't. Select one to open [the sub-agent](#a-sub-agent) |
| **+9** at an edge | Nodes past the edge; select it to slide them into view |

Select a node to show it in the inspector. Double-click it, or press **Enter**, to open its own view at `/work-items/<id>/nodes/<node>`, which draws the tasks inside it; **← chain** goes back. Drag or scroll to pan, pinch or **⌘**-scroll to zoom. **Fit** shows the whole chain.

### A slice group

_Added in next_

When a plan is [sliced](/concepts/how-a-work-item-runs#slices), the chain draws its implementation and the nodes after it as one **slices** node. The words under it say where the group is: **shared groundwork** while the item builds the tasks in no slice, how many slices are done, an amber line for one that needs you or failed, and which slice the **join** replays. Its own view draws:

- **groundwork**: the nodes the item runs on the shared groundwork.
- **slices**: one box per slice, in its own item's state. Double-click one to open that item.
- **join**: the slices' commits replayed onto the item's branch, in slice order.

A slice's page puts its parent in the crumbs; the **▾** beside the slice lists the parent and every slice with its state. Once a slice is archived its worktree is gone, but its page still shows its changed files, read from where the join replayed them. Its own gate attempts and reviews aren't there, and once the item is archived too neither page shows changes.

### The chain overview

_Added in 2.1_

A chain too long to read whole has an overview above it: the chain drawn small on one line, each node in its state: done, running, waiting on you in amber, failed or not started. A frame marks the part the graph shows; select a node to move the graph there. A chain that fits whole, such as `quick-task`, has none. The [Chains](/reference/web-ui/templates#chains) editor draws one the same way.

## The inspector

The panel on the right shows whatever you selected: the chain, a node, a gate or a task. **Collapse pane**, or **Esc**, folds it away. Config values [before the item starts](/reference/web-ui/config-before-start) and a [node's fix loop](/reference/web-ui/fix-loops-and-test-scopes#a-node-with-a-fix-loop) have pages of their own.

### The chain

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Status, progress, the node it is at, the attached spec or plan, spend, the checkout of an [in-place item](/guides/run/run-items-in-place) and recent events. A `one-off chain` row says who approved a [one-off chain](/guides/day-to-day/file-a-work-item#file-with-a-one-off-chain)'s custom content, or that nobody has yet |
| **Config** | Its limits and overrides. While the item can take a draft, the **+** on a connector in the chain adds a node or gate |

### A node

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Progress, what runs next, spend and its steps, with **Retry** once the item has stopped. The node a failed item stopped on has it on the state card instead; a paused item offers **Resume** |
| **Config** | Its settings |

**Focus** opens the node's own view.

### A task

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Thread** | On the escalation task only: the conversation up to the turn you picked |
| **Overview** | The [harness](/concepts/vocabulary#harness) and model the attempt ran on, and its tokens and cost. A **Sub-agents** list while Claude Code sub-agents run. On the implementing task, progress through the plan's tasks _Added in next_ |
| **Input** | What went in: the head commit, the fix-loop round it carries findings from (or "first pass"), the escalation thread and a pending [steer](/concepts/vocabulary#steer) |
| **Output** | The session's summary note. On a task that writes a document, **wrote** opens it |
| **Log** | The agent session's log. An **agent** menu filters to one sub-agent's lines _Added in next_ |
| **Config** | The attempt's own values, read-only. Before the item starts, [the draft's overrides](/reference/web-ui/config-before-start) |

A token figure reads as two parts when the run read from the prompt cache, such as `40k new + 900k cached`. **cached** is what was read back, which costs a fraction of fresh input; **new** is everything else. Hover the figure for all four counts. **new** grows when a session ends, since output is known only then.

A task that ran more than once reads **attempt n of m ▾** under its name: pick an attempt for every tab to show. The tabs follow the newest attempt until you pick an earlier one.

### A sub-agent

_Added in next_

A Claude Code sub-agent opens a level under its task from its mark on the graph, its row in **Overview** or the **agent** menu on **Log**. **Overview** shows its tokens, tool uses, what it runs now or its answer, and its prompt. **Log** shows only its lines. The task's crumb, or **All** in the Log's menu, goes back.

## A gate that is waiting

The panel of a waiting gate shows:

- the files changed so far and the open review threads;
- **Concerns**: what a task said it doubted about its own work since the last gate you decided;
- the document it decides on, and where a rejection sends the work;
- rows: **tests** (`✓ 3 scopes`, or `✗ 1 of 3 scopes` with each red scope linked to its log), **decides on**, **reject to** and **status**.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Read** and the document's name | Opens the document beside the panel |
| **Review gate** | Opens the review page at this gate |
| **Approve**, **Request changes…** | Decide the gate. A must-fix thread open refuses **Approve**. **Request changes…** names the node the work goes back to, and stays off until you write a note |

### The gate's reviewer

_Added in 2.5_

A gate with an [`auto_review`](/guides/customize/add-review-agents) reviewer draws it beside the gate, with a chip for its latest outcome:

| Chip | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **approve**, **reject** | The reviewer's decision |
| **ready for you** | It checked and left the decision to you |
| **could not decide** | It couldn't reach a verdict |
| **runs first when reached** | The item hasn't reached the gate yet |

When Kraft left a note on the outcome, the gate's panel shows it under **Reviewer: ready for you** or **Reviewer: could not decide**.

### A gate's rounds

_Added in next_

A gate that was asked more than once, because you sent the work back and the chain came round again, keeps every round. A round starts when the chain reaches the gate and ends when someone decides it, a skip passes it, or the item ends.

With two or more rounds, a round picker sits at the foot of the gate's view, as a node has one for its passes. Pick a round to see the gate as that round left it: its state, the reviewer's verdict for that round, the note it was decided with, and where it sent the work. **latest ↩** goes back to the newest round. The round is in the address as `?pass=`.

Once a round has ended, the gate's panel lists a **History** of its rounds, below the gate's facts, newest first. A gate that is waiting for its first answer has none, because the status line says the same.

Each row shows the round's number, when it ended, how it ended (**approved**, **changes requested**, **fixed**, **skipped**, **closed** or **waiting**) and who decided it, but none of its note. A **now** marks a round still waiting. Select a row to pick that round: the row you picked is marked.

The gate's view previews the round's note: its first paragraph, rendered as Markdown and clipped at three lines. When the note has more than that, a **Read the note** button under the preview opens the whole note, rendered, in the document drawer, titled `<gate> · round N note`. Closing the drawer puts focus back on the button.

A round that holds a review or at least one review thread is a review round. Kraft decides that from the round's data, not from the gate's kind. A finished review round shows its review beside the note: the summary with a **Read the review** button, a count line such as `3 threads · 2 must-fix · 1 question · 1 open`, and its first three threads (must-fix first, then oldest), each as one line of Markdown.

The review's own outcome (**approved**, **changes requested** or **commented**) shows only when it differs from how the round ended, which the badge already says.

The note shows beside a review round unless it was the review-thread bounce's: Kraft's own rejection of a gate with open threads, whose note is those threads as text. A note a person wrote when requesting changes always shows, because it's what the next agent was told.

The pending round shows no review block. A History row also reads the count line, and the row you picked has an **Open in review** link of its own.

**Open in review** opens that round on the review page, at `?gate=` and `?round=`. It shows the round's threads with the lines they were made on, their comments and their state. It is read-only, and it works after the gate has passed. It shows no diff.

A round holds the review and the threads made after the gate's previous round ended, up to the end of this one. A thread opened between two rounds belongs to the later round. A thread's state is its state now.

While the gate isn't waiting for you, its badge and the panel's subtitle say how the newest round ended, such as **changes requested · you**.

| Who decided | Reads as |
|---|---|
| You | **you** |
| The assistant, over MCP | **assistant** |
| An agent reviewer, or Kraft itself | **auto** |

A past round can't be decided. While one is shown, the panel has no **Approve**, **Request changes…** or **Read** button, and no changed files or **decides on** row, because those describe the gate now. A round Kraft closed without asking you, such as an unchanged chain revision or a bounce on open review threads, has no **asked** time.
