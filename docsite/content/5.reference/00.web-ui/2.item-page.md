title: An item's page
description: What an item's page shows, from the header's main action to the chain graph and the inspector for a chain, node, gate or task.
---

An item's page shows one [work item](/concepts/vocabulary#work-item): its brief, its [chain](/concepts/vocabulary#chain) drawn as a graph, and an inspector for whatever you select. This page names the header's actions, the graph and the inspector's tabs, one heading for each part.

![An item's page: the chain drawn as a graph with the spec_approval gate selected, and the inspector on the right showing the gate's files, open threads, and Approve and Reject buttons](/assets/item.png)

## The page

An item's page, at `/work-items/<id>`, is under **Board › repo › item** in
the header: **Board** goes back to the board, and the repo opens the board
filtered to that repo. The page has:

- its title and brief at the top (**edit** changes the brief);
- the size of the change so far, with a **Review changes** link;
- a banner when the item needs you, such as **Waiting for your approval at
  `spec_approval`** with **Review changes**, which opens the review page at that
  [gate](/concepts/vocabulary#gate) with the gate's brief beside the diff.

The line under the brief counts the changed files and, when there are any, the
open [review threads](/concepts/vocabulary#review-thread).

## The main action

The header at the top right shows how long ago the item was created (or how
long it ran, once it has ended), its status, and the main action, which is
always one the item can take:

| The item | Main action |
|---|---|
| never started | **Start** |
| running or waiting | **Pause** |
| paused | **Resume** |
| at a gate | **Open gate**, which selects the gate (on the review page, opens its document) |
| asking a question | **Answer**, which goes to the answer box |
| stopped at a [cap](/concepts/vocabulary#cap) or a budget it can raise | **Raise cap**, which opens the editor for the limit the stop names, or Config's budget editor for the item's own dollar cap. A cap that names no limit opens Config. [Which stop offers what](/guides/day-to-day/raise-a-cap#which-cap-stopped-the-item) |
| [stuck](/concepts/vocabulary#stuck), failed, or with an escalation running | **Retry** |
| stopped on a rebase conflict | **Review conflicts** |
| stopped because its MR was closed | **Reopen MR** |
| done or cancelled | **Archive** |
| archived | **Restore** |

An item that never started reads **NOT STARTED**, here and on the phone, never **Paused**.

## Header actions

The header has two menus, and some actions that ask before they act.

### The menus

Clicking the main action's name runs it. Its **▾**, or the pointer resting on
it, opens a menu over it with the main action first and then the others that
fit the item's state: **Escalate…**, **Mark complete…**, **Archive** and
**Cancel…**. Each of those that asks for more opens a card under the button.

The **⋮** menu holds **Review changes**, **Item settings**, **Open [worktree](/concepts/vocabulary#worktree) in
editor**, **Copy ID** and **Copy link**, and **Duplicate as new item** once
the item has ended.

Click the title to rename the item: **Enter** or clicking away saves, **Esc**
cancels.

### Escalate…

**Escalate…** is offered only on an item stopped or paused after it started.
It is not offered at a stop on a dollar or token budget (raise the cap first),
nor while an agent session is running on the item's pending gate, nor while an
escalation is already running.

Once the item has an escalation thread, it offers **Start a new thread** and
says what it changes: it continues the last thread, which remembers its earlier
turns, or starts a fresh one that does not see it.

### Mark complete… and Cancel…

Both say, before you confirm:

- what they stop (when something runs: the running attempt and how long it has
  run, whose work is lost);
- what they keep (the branch, the worktree, findings and the run log);
- what the status becomes.

## The chain graph

The middle of the page draws the item's chain, frozen when the item was filed,
[node](/concepts/vocabulary#node) by node. A box is an agent or a command (an exec node); a diamond is a
gate. Each node shows how it went and how long it took, and a gate that sends
work back draws a **reject** arc to the node it reruns.

A node that ran more than once carries a **×N** badge: the most times any one
of its tasks ran, whether by a retry or a fix-loop round. A changed-test-scope
task counts its rounds, not the scopes in them. The badge is red on a node
stopped at its cap.

- Click a node to show it in the inspector.
- Double-click a node, or press **Enter** on it, to open its own view at
  `/work-items/<id>/nodes/<node>`, which draws the tasks inside the node.
  **← chain** goes back.
- Drag to pan. The controls at the bottom right zoom in and out, fit the
  chain to the window (down to 30%: a longer chain than that still pans), and
  center on the current node.

### Where the graph opens

It depends on the item:

- **An item that has not started** opens at a readable size, never under 80%,
  starting from its first node; pan to see the rest.
- **A failed or finished item** opens fitted but never under 45%, and a chain
  too long for that opens on the node it stopped on, or its last node.

## The inspector

The panel on the right of the graph shows whatever you selected: the chain,
a node, a gate or a task. Its heading names it. **Collapse pane** at the top
right, or **Esc**, folds it away. **Esc** leaves it open while the cursor is in
a box that holds text, so the text is not thrown away.

A [gate that is waiting](#a-gate-that-is-waiting) has a heading of its own.
The Config values [before the item starts](/reference/web-ui/config-before-start)
have a page of their own, as do a [node's fix loop](/reference/web-ui/fix-loops-and-test-scopes#a-node-with-a-fix-loop)
and a [changed-test-scope task](/reference/web-ui/fix-loops-and-test-scopes#a-changed-test-scope-task).

### The chain

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | the item's status, progress, the node it is at, the spec or plan attached when it was filed, what it has spent, and its recent events. Click an [attachment](/concepts/vocabulary#attachment)'s name to read it, even before the item starts |
| **Config** | its limits and overrides, as in the side panel on [The board](/reference/web-ui/board#the-side-panel) |

### A node

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | its progress, what runs next, what it spent and its steps, with **Retry** to rerun it once the item has stopped |
| **Config** | its settings |

A paused item offers **Resume** in Overview instead of Retry, and a running or
waiting one has no Retry. **Focus**, at the top right of a node's panel on the
chain, opens the node's own view. Clicking a step, or a step's task, opens that
view with it selected.

### A task

A task has the tabs **Overview**, **Input**, **Output**, **Log** and **Config**. The escalation task has a sixth, **Thread**, before them.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Thread** | on the escalation task, first of its tabs: the conversation up to the turn you picked |
| **Overview** | the [harness](/concepts/vocabulary#harness) and model the attempt ran on |
| **Input** | what the API keeps of what went in: the head commit, the fix-loop round it carries findings from (or "first pass"), the escalation thread, and a pending [steer](/concepts/vocabulary#steer) |
| **Output** | the session's own summary note, led by **wrote** on a task that writes a document |
| **Log** | the agent session's own log; **full screen** spreads it over the whole window, and **Esc** brings it back |
| **Config** | the attempt's own values, read-only: its path, model, attempt number and head commit. Before the item starts, [the draft's overrides](/reference/web-ui/config-before-start) |

Two tabs show more on some tasks:

- **Overview on the task that implements the plan** also shows how far through
  the plan's sub-tasks (its `## Task N` headings) the work is, with the commit
  that finished each one and a link to the plan.
- **Output on a task that writes a document** (its `produces`, such as the work
  brief) leads with **wrote**, which opens that document, above the session's own
  summary note. It is on the newest attempt only, once that attempt is done,
  since each attempt rewrites the one file, and not on an escalation, a gate's
  `auto_review` or a [fix loop](/concepts/vocabulary#fix-loop)'s repair and judge.

### A task that ran more than once

The subtitle under its name reads **attempt n of m ▾**. Click it for a menu of
every attempt, newest first, each with how it went and how long it ran, and pick
the one every tab shows.

The tabs follow the newest attempt as it starts. Pick an earlier one and they
stay on it, while the count shows the newer ones; pick the newest again to
follow.

On the escalation the menu counts turns, **turn n of m ▾**, as its **Thread**
tab does.

### What Esc does on the page

One **Esc** steps the page back one level, in this order:

1. Whatever is open over the page takes it first: the review, a picker, the
   escalation card, the full-screen log or a document.
2. Then the inspector folds.
3. Then a node's own view goes back to the chain.

A box that holds text, and an open menu or dialog, keep their own **Esc**. In a
[changed-test-scope task](/reference/web-ui/fix-loops-and-test-scopes#stepping-back-and-closing),
**Esc** first steps back from a scope to its task, and from the task to its box.

## A gate that is waiting

The panel of a waiting gate shows:

- the files changed so far;
- the open review threads;
- the document it decides on;
- where a rejection sends the work.

At the bottom:

- **Read** followed by the document's name opens it in a panel on the right
  (**full screen** widens it).
- **Review changes** opens the review page.
- **Approve** and **Reject…** decide the gate.
