The Analytics screen rolls up where the time and the money went across your [work items](/concepts/vocabulary#work-item). This page names each panel, figure and control, and says what each number counts. Open it from **Analytics** in the sidebar.

![The Analytics screen for items created in the last 8 weeks: the Repo and Chain filters, the Completed, Lead time and Cost figures with a line of detail under each, the Throughput by week panel (empty in this sample, which merged nothing), and the top of the By node table](/assets/analytics.png)

## What the screen counts

- **The items.** Every figure covers the work items created in the last 8 weeks (56 days), narrowed by the two filters. An item created earlier is left out, even if it merged yesterday. The header reads **Items created in the last 8 weeks**. Only **Completed**, **Lead time**, the share waiting on you and the two figures under the stops are limited to completed items. Every other figure covers any status.
- **Their history.** For each of those items, the screen adds up all of its [worker sessions](/concepts/vocabulary#worker-session) and all of its events, whenever they happened.
- **No stored totals.** Kraft computes the figures from the database each time you open the screen or change a filter.

## Filters

Two menus sit at the right of the header. Both default to `all`, and both apply to every panel.

| Menu | Choices | Narrows to |
|---|---|---|
| **Repo ▾** | **All repos**, then each repo by its folder name, with the number of your work items beside it. | The items filed in that repo. |
| **Chain ▾** | **All chains**, then each [chain](/concepts/vocabulary#chain) your work items use, with the number of items beside it. | The items on that chain. An item that never chose one counts as `default`. |

The filters reset to `all` when you leave the screen.

## Overview

Three figures, each with a line of detail under it.

| Figure | What it counts | The line under it |
|---|---|---|
| **Completed** | Items whose status is `completed`. | `+N vs previous` (or `-N`): the change from the 8 weeks before, counted the same way. `$X cost ÷ completed`: **Cost** divided by **Completed**, shown when **Completed** is not `0`. |
| **Lead time** | The median, over completed items, of creation to completion, so a wait after the merge, such as `post_merge_ci`, counts. | `median create → completion`. `N% waiting on you`: the time completed items spent stopped on you, as a share of their lead time. |
| **Cost** | The cost recorded for every [worker](/concepts/vocabulary#worker) session of every item in the set, completed or not. | `N rounds per item that ran a fix-loop node`, `N capped` and `N rejected gates`, explained below. |

The figures use the same units as the rest of the UI:

- A duration reads `45s`, `12m`, `2h 5m` or `1d 3h`.
- A dollar amount has three decimals under $1 (`$0.116`) and two from $1 (`$1.16`).
- A token count reads `850`, `17k` or `1.2M`.

Time waiting on you runs from the moment a gate is requested, or an item stops and waits for you, until the gate is approved or rejected, the item is retried, or a [node](/concepts/vocabulary#node) starts. It is kept apart from the item's run time, so a slow reviewer does not read as a slow agent.

### Fix rounds, capped and rejected gates

- **N rounds per item that ran a fix-loop node** is the count of distinct rounds that ran a worker session in the items' [fix-loop](/concepts/vocabulary#fix-loop) nodes, divided by the number of items with at least one. It covers every node that declares a fix loop, whatever its name.
- A node's first pass is round `0` and counts only where it ran a session. In the shipped `default` chain, `merge_request_feedback` waits on CI and review without one, so it adds a round only when a repair runs. The repair pass that precedes the first paid cycle is no round and does not count.
- **N capped** is the number of items with a fix-loop session that ended at its attempt limit. A wait that ran out and a time [cap](/concepts/vocabulary#cap) do not count.
- **N rejected gates** is the number of times a [gate](/concepts/vocabulary#gate) was rejected on these items, by you or by Kraft's own review.

### A trailing plus

A trailing `+` (`$1.16+`) marks a sum that is missing a session: one with tokens and no dollar figure. For example, the [harness](/concepts/vocabulary#harness) reports no cost and its model is not in Kraft's price table, or a resumed session's tokens could not be separated from the earlier session's. The true total is higher. The session's tokens still count in **By node**. For which harnesses report no cost, see [Cost reporting](/reference/harnesses/cost-reporting).

Kraft's own estimate from the price table also goes into the sum, when the model is listed. A session still running adds a live estimate, on any harness, `claude` included. A finished session whose agent reported no cost adds a final one: a harness that never reports one, or a crash or interruption before the agent reported. The sum includes them unmarked, and the notes under **Overview** and **By node** say so.

A running session's live estimate leaves out output tokens, so it is a floor.

## Throughput by week

A bar chart of merged items per week: eight columns, oldest first. The number above a bar is its count, and a bar is as tall as its count against the tallest week. The label under it is the Monday that starts the week, as month/day, in UTC. The last bar, drawn dashed, is the current week and is partial.

A merge here is a node finishing that runs a [forge](/concepts/vocabulary#forge) task with the target `mr.merge`, such as the shipped `merge` node. A completed item whose chain has no such node, the shipped `quick-task` chain for one, completes without showing up. The note under the chart says so. When nothing merged, the chart is replaced with **Nothing merged in this range.**

## By node

One row per node that ran a worker session for these items, highest cost first.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **node** | The node's id in its chain. |
| **share of cost** | A bar: the node's cost against the costliest node, which fills the bar. |
| **min** | The mean wall time of one worker session on the node, over every item in range, in whole minutes. Under 30 seconds shows `0`. |
| **tokens** | Every token its sessions used: uncached input, cache writes, cache reads and output. |
| **$** | The cost of its sessions. A trailing `+` marks a missing session, as under [Cost](#a-trailing-plus). |
| **%** | The node's **$** as a share of all rows' **$**, rounded. `0%` when the total is `0`. |

The note under the table reads "Minutes are the mean wall time of one worker session, over every item in range."

With no sessions, the panel reads **Nothing here yet.**

## By repo

One row per repo, the one with the most items first.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **repo** | The repo's folder name. |
| **items** | Items created in the last 8 weeks, in any status. |
| **done** | Those items whose status is `completed`. |
| **cost** | The cost of their sessions. A trailing `+` marks a missing session. |
| **avg deepest fix round** | The average, over the repo's items that ran a worker session, of the deepest round any of the item's nodes reached. The first pass is round `0`. It is not the **Overview**'s count of rounds. |

## Why items stopped for a person

One bar per reason, with its count, most common first. A count is a number of stops, not of items: an item that stops three times adds three. Every item in the set counts, completed or not.

| Label | A stop where |
|---|---|
| `gate · <name>` | A gate named `<name>` was requested. |
| `budget` | A spend cap stopped the item, and the stop carries the budget's figures. |
| `cap breach` | A [fix loop](/concepts/vocabulary#fix-loop) reached its attempt cap, and the stop carries its cycle and attempt counts. |
| `orchestrator/infra defect` | The reason starts `resume: current-node session did not resolve cleanly`. |
| `preventable dispatch` | The reason starts `needs_context:` and contains `blocked on`, `not merged` or `depends on`, in any case. |
| `task failure` | Any other stop that waits on you, including one no earlier label fits. |

For what each stop means and its fix, see [Why did my item stop?](/troubleshooting/why-did-my-item-stop). With no stops, the panel reads **Nothing stopped for a person in this range.**

Under the bars, one line holds two more figures, both over completed items only:

- **N unplanned touches per item**, to two decimals. It adds up the stops that nobody designed the chain to need and divides by the completed items. A `gate · ...` stop never counts. `budget`, `orchestrator/infra defect` and `preventable dispatch` always count. `cap breach` and `task failure` count, unless Kraft cleared the stop without paging you. Two cases clear it: an automatic escalation retry, and a gate that Kraft's own review decided.
- **open MR → checks done X median**: the median time from the node that opens the draft merge request starting to the node that waits on CI completing. `0s` shows when no item has both.

A node is found by its task, never by its id: a forge task targeting `mr.open_draft` opens the merge request, one targeting `mr.ci` waits on CI. On the shipped `default` chain these are `draft_merge_request` and `merge_request_feedback`. The span starts when the node begins, with the rebase, and ends when CI, the automated review and any repair rounds have all passed. It covers completed items only. An item whose chain lacks either node, such as one on `quick-task`, adds nothing.

## On a phone

On a phone, **Analytics** is a tab in the bottom bar and the same screen at `/analytics`, laid out as four tiles and three lists. See [Kraft on a phone](/guides/day-to-day/kraft-on-a-phone).

- **Range** at the top right picks **7 days** (the default), **30 days** or **90 days**, by the same created-in-range rule. There is no Repo or Chain filter, and no weekly chart.
- **Spent** is **Cost**, with `$X spent ÷ completed` under it, the same quotient as on the desktop, and **Items done** is **Completed**, with how many items are running now. That count includes escalated items and is not limited to the range.
- **Time at gates** is the total time stopped on you across all items in the range; under it, how much of their lead time completed items spent waiting on you. **Fix loops** is **rounds per item that ran a fix-loop node**, with how many items hit their cap.
- **By node** shows each node's runs and cost, **By repo** shows items, done and avg deepest fix round with the cost, and **Why items stopped for a person** is the same list as above, with the same line under it for unplanned touches and open MR → checks done.

When nothing has completed in the range and no node ran, the lists give way to **Nothing has completed in this range yet.**

## The numbers behind it

The screen reads `GET /api/analytics` with `range`, `repo` and `template` (the chain). `range` is `7d`, `30d`, `90d`, `8w` or `all`, and `7d` when omitted; any other value is refused with a 400. The desktop screen always asks for `8w`. The phone asks for `7d`, `30d` or `90d`.
