Pick the page that matches what you are seeing.

- [Why did my item stop?](/troubleshooting/why-did-my-item-stop): a work item sits under **Needs you** with a reason, or a merge request waits on CI or a review.
- [Starting and reaching Kraft](/troubleshooting/starting-and-reaching-kraft): `kraft: command not found`, a server that will not start because its port is taken, or a 403 "unexpected Host" from a webhook or proxy.
- [Doctor failures](/troubleshooting/doctor-failures): a `kraft admin doctor` row that fails or warns.
- [FAQ](/troubleshooting/faq): short answers on forges, harnesses, cost, platforms, specs, Kraft Lite and the permission gate.

After an upgrade from 1.4, a page that is not where you left it or a theme that looks different is covered in
[Where did a page go, and why does my theme look different?](/guides/run/upgrade-kraft#where-did-a-page-go-and-why-does-my-theme-look-different)
