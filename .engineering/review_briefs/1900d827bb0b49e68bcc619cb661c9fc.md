## What changed

Three independent, arithmetically-wrong numbers on the board, each fixed and tested:

- **Kraft-g2pi** (`src/kraft/analytics.py`, `frontend/src/views/Analytics.tsx`,
  `frontend/src/types.ts`): `analytics.compute` now emits `totals["work_items_run"]`
  — items with at least one `worker_sessions` row — and the Cost tile divides by
  that instead of every item in the date range (including ones that never ran).
  Tile relabelled "per work item run" to match.
- **Kraft-7co4** (`src/kraft/reattach.py`): `_exit_from_file` now takes a
  `log_path` and calls `usage.read(log_path, result_path)`, so an adopted
  session records model/tokens/cost instead of leaving those columns NULL.
- **Kraft-ao6** (`plugins/kraft-lite/kl.py`): `since()` now pairs each timestamp
  with its record's walk position instead of sorting by timestamp alone, so two
  nodes closed within the same second keep their walk order rather than one
  falling back to the epic and being billed the whole run.

Tests added: `tests/test_analytics.py`, `tests/test_reattach.py`,
`frontend/src/views/Analytics.test.tsx` (updated), `plugins/kraft-lite/tests/test_records.py`.
All four suites pass locally (18 + 7 + 29 backend/frontend/kraft-lite tests).
