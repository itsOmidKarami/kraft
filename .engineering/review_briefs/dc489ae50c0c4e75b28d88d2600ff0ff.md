## What changed

Implements the rate-limit auto-retry design end to end:

- `src/kraft/subprocess.py`: detects a rejected `rate_limit_event` in an agent's stream-json log.
- `src/kraft/executor.py`: on detection, stops the node without running the fix loop and schedules `retry_at`.
- `src/kraft/store.py`: `mark_rate_limited`, and clears `retry_at` on `needs_human`/`retry` transitions.
- `src/kraft/policy.py` (or equivalent): `rate_limit_retries` cap.
- `src/kraft/api.py`: auto-relaunches a rate-limited work item once `retry_at` passes.
