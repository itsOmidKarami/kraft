53 AUTOMATED_REVIEW_SETTLED = "automated_review_settled" 

54 #: Kraft is about to write a reply or the summary comment: recorded before any write. 

55 REPLY_INTENDED = "reply_intended" 

56 #: Recorded just before a write: if no outcome follows, the send reads as unclear and 

57 #: is reconciled. Not a failure and not a counted send. 

58 REPLY_SEND_STARTED = "reply_send_started" 

59 #: A send that did not post: it may have landed (`unclear`), hit a rate limit or 

60 #: cannot succeed now (`deferred`). 

61 REPLY_SEND_FAILED = "reply_send_failed" 

62 #: The write landed, a reconcile found its copy, or a duplicate check moved it. 

63 REPLY_POSTED = "reply_posted" 

64 #: A reconcile read the thread and found no copy of an unclear write. 

65 REPLY_NOT_FOUND = "reply_not_found" 

66 #: The intent will never be sent: refused, superseded or given up. 

67 REPLY_ENDED = "reply_ended" 

68 #: A duplicate check deleted a later copy of a reply and kept the earliest. 

69 REPLY_DUPLICATE_DELETED = "reply_duplicate_deleted" 

70 #: A node's review wait names a bot with replies on, but nothing in the node can 

71 #: act on a reply (no fix loop, no triage, a multi-repo item), the forge cannot 

72 #: be resolved, or Kraft writes as one of the bots. Once per node run and reason. 

73 REPLIES_INERT = "replies_inert" 

74 MERGE_QUEUE_DROPPED = "merge_queue_dropped" 

75 #: Kraft rewrote the head the final review approved itself -- a clean 

76 #: rebase, a pointer bump -- and pinned the new one (`kraft.reviewed_head`). 

77 REVIEWED_HEAD_REPINNED = "reviewed_head_repinned" 

78 #: The merge would have pushed a head the final review did not approve. 

79 MERGE_REFUSED_UNREVIEWED = "merge_refused_unreviewed" 

80 #: The forge merged a head the final review did not approve: Kraft asked for 

81 #: the merge, and a commit reached the merge request before it landed. 

82 MERGE_LANDED_UNREVIEWED = "merge_landed_unreviewed"
